Our Story

We Create Art
From Your Love

Eternity Studio was born from a deep passion for preserving life's most beautiful moments. Founded in 2014 by Arjun Mehta, a celebrated wedding photographer, our studio has grown into one of India's most sought-after wedding photography brands.

With over a decade of experience, we've mastered the art of transforming fleeting seconds into timeless treasures. Our approach blends editorial sophistication with authentic emotion, ensuring every photograph reflects the genuine connection between two people in love.

We believe that wedding photography is more than just documentation — it's about creating heirlooms that will be cherished for generations. Every image we create is crafted with intention, artistry, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.
